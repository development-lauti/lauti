Tour

From a blank app to your first story in three steps.

  1. Scan a card

    Hold an NFC tag near the top of your phone to scan it. Once scanned, the tag shows up as a card in your set.

  2. Add audio

    Card detected! Tap the card to open it and add audio: a local file, a podcast feed, or a track from your own music server. Then go back to the Cards page.

  3. Start Kids Mode

    All set! Start Kids Mode and hand the phone to your child. Every linked card now plays on touch. To leave Kids Mode, press your four secret buttons in order – set the sequence under Extras → Password.

Tip: group cards into sets such as "Bedtime" or "Car Trips" and give each set its own maximum volume, playback order and repeat mode.
