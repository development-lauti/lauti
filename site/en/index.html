Tap a card, hear a story.

Lauti turns any NFC card into a play button for your child's favorite stories, music, and podcasts. No screen, no menus, no ads – just tap a card and listen.

How it works

Link a physical NFC card or tag to a playlist. In Kids Mode, tapping a linked card starts playback immediately – nothing else to press or navigate. Parents manage cards, playlists, and settings in the regular app view, protected by a simple 4-button password.

Screen-free listening

Kids Mode is a full-screen, distraction-free lock screen with only the essentials visible. Children can pick and play their own audio independently, without unsupervised access to a phone or tablet.

Flexible audio sources

  • Local files – add audio already stored on your device
  • RSS / podcast feeds – play the latest or a random episode automatically
  • Music server – connect to your own Subsonic-compatible server (Navidrome, Airsonic, Gonic, and others) and stream your personal library

Organize with sets

Group cards into themed sets – "Bedtime," "Car Trips," "Learning" – each with its own volume cap, playback order (in order or shuffle), and repeat mode.

Parental controls

  • 4-button password to exit Kids Mode
  • Per-set maximum volume, so playback never gets too loud
  • Full playback history with a weekly top 5, so you always know what's been played

Privacy by design

Lauti works entirely offline for local files, with no account, no ads, and no tracking or analytics of any kind. Data you create (cards, playlists, history) stays on your device. Internet access is only used when you choose to use a podcast feed, connect your own music server, or make a voluntary in-app donation. Read the full privacy policy.

Who it's for

Parents who want to give their kids independence with audio content – without handing over a screen. Perfect for bedtime stories, car rides, quiet time, and building a love of listening.
